Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Longview Restoration Co sends flood cleanup crews throughout Longview to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Longview

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating

Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. For emergency water extraction in Longview, Longview Restoration Co brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Program (708) 391-5296 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Sprinkler discharge that has left water on floors and contents
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas

What a Professional Brings to Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.

Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
